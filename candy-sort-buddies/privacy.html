Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data the game Candy Sort Buddies (the "Game") handles. The Game is made by Blue Lobster Studios, an independent developer based in Portugal ("we"). Contact: pageonetwitch@gmail.com.

In short: no account and no purchases. We never ask for your name, e-mail or contacts. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID, subject to your consent where the law requires it. We collect anonymous gameplay statistics to improve the Game, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Anonymous stats. Your progress is stored only on your device.

1. Game progress

Your levels, stars, chosen buddy and settings (volume, language) are saved only on your device. We don't receive them. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy settings link in the main menu. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads). See how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Anonymous gameplay statistics (PostHog)

To find which levels are too hard, where players stop and which bugs happen, the Game sends anonymous gameplay events to PostHog, hosted on servers in the European Union. These include level started, won, stuck or left, number of moves, time taken, stars, the use of the extra tube, buddies unlocked, errors, and ads shown.

Each event also carries:

  • a random ID created when the Game is first opened. It is not linked to your identity, advertising ID or Google account;
  • the app version, device model, operating system version, screen size, the Game's language and the region of your phone's language setting.

We don't use your IP address to locate you, and our analytics project discards it.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the Game. You can object at any time by switching off Settings → Anonymous stats. The Game then stops collecting and deletes events not yet sent.

Statistics are kept for up to 12 months.

4. Who receives data

Only the providers above:

  • Google (advertising);
  • PostHog Inc. (analytics, EU hosting);
  • Google Play, which also processes data when you download and update apps under its own policy;
  • itch.io, which hosts the browser version and processes data under its own policy.

We don't sell personal data.

5. Children

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you have the right to:

  • access your data;
  • have it rectified or deleted;
  • object to or restrict its processing;
  • data portability.

Because the statistics are pseudonymous, we may be unable to link them to you without more information. Switching off Anonymous stats and resetting your advertising ID are the quickest ways to stop processing. To exercise your rights, write to pageonetwitch@gmail.com.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal, that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt).

7. Security

Data sent by the Game travels encrypted (HTTPS).

8. Playing in a browser (itch.io)

The browser version differs from the mobile app in these ways:

  • It shows no ads, so section 2 doesn't apply and Google AdMob isn't used.
  • Your progress, settings and the random ID from section 3 are saved in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing the site's data deletes them, and the Game then counts as newly installed.
  • The anonymous statistics in section 3 work the same way and can be switched off the same way. The region comes from your browser's language setting.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be announced on the Game's store or itch.io page.
